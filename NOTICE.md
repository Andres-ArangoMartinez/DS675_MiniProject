# Sources and redistribution

The student research direction and AI assistance are described in [the contribution statement](docs/student_contributions.md). The submitted course reports retain their authorship. No blanket license is asserted over third-party articles, data or author repositories.

BattLeDIM data are credited to Vrachimis et al. and distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); see the [official record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4017659). Downloaded data are kept outside tracked source files.

The [paper catalog](literature/papers_catalog.csv) identifies each document, original source, license, public-copy path and SHA-256. PDFs are included only where the recovered evidence establishes redistribution permission; bytes remain unmodified. License evidence includes official record metadata and printed PDF license statements. CC BY-NC-ND material remains unmodified and retains attribution. This is a noncommercial academic repository. Other papers are accessible through links to their official sources.

Conference reports and journal successors retain their separate document identities, licenses and results. The paper archive also retains supporting and excluded background material; only `source_records.csv` and `groups.csv` define the historical 47-source / 35-group denominator.
