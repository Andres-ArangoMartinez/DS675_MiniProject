# Sources and redistribution

[Student/AI attribution](docs/student_contributions.md) identifies project contributions. Third-party articles, data and repositories retain their respective source and license declarations.

Vrachimis et al.'s [official BattLeDIM record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4017659) declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded inputs remain outside Git tracking.

The [paper catalog](literature/papers_catalog.csv) records source links, declared licenses, public-copy paths, SHA-256 fingerprints and supporting evidence. Retained evidence comprises official metadata, supporting license records and printed PDF statements. The 32 public PDFs match their recorded fingerprints; CC BY-NC-ND copies retain unmodified bytes and attribution in this noncommercial academic repository. These records document the inclusion basis rather than independent legal certification. Other papers have official-source links.

Conference and journal documents retain separate identities, licenses and reported results. Supporting/background material is cataloged separately from the historical **47 source records assigned to 35 related-work groups**, defined by `source_records.csv` and `groups.csv`.
