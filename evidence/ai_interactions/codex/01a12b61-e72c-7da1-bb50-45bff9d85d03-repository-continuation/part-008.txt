## Source and calculations

The pipeline verifies the eleven files in the [official-source manifest](../data/source_manifest.json): eight annual SCADA tables, the nominal model, sensor configuration and README. It checks full 2018 and 2019 table structure, but computes pressure forecasts/errors and correlations only for **January–September 2018**. Table checks do not establish that 2019 was never previously inspected.

[Data loading and calculations](../src/analysis.py) use the six selected signals, without interpolation, filtering or scaling. The period is cropped before `n1.shift(-3)` creates the future label. The last three origins cannot obtain labels inside September and are dropped. This produces 78,621 pairs from 78,624 timestamps. These pairs use current inputs for descriptive correlations and persistence; they are **not** the later lagged training matrix, which will also require 55 minutes of history.

For error `e = n1(u + 15) − n1(u)`, MAE is the mean of `abs(e)` and RMSE is the square root of the mean of `e²`. Pearson correlations use the same aligned pairs. The future target is never an input predictor.
