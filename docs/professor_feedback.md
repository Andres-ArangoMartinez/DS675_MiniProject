## Additional Clarification of the Anticipated Contribution

The C1–C3 contributions, originally defined in Milestone 2, propose a reproducible evaluation of 15-minute-ahead pressure forecasting at node n1, comparing established regression models against the persistence baseline and examining the incremental predictive value of auxiliary hydraulic signals from Area C. In response to the feedback received, it is important to clarify that the anticipated contribution is not to develop a new algorithm, but rather to determine whether additional hydraulic information significantly improves forecast accuracy and what insights can be gained regarding the usefulness of these measurements.

A subsequent review of the relatively small persistence error revealed an additional operational limitation: its performance depends on the availability of reliable and up-to-date measurements from the target sensor. This observation motivates a possible extension to investigate whether a model based exclusively on the five auxiliary signals can provide useful forecasts when the n1 measurement is unavailable or compromised. Additionally, temporal and cross-sensor consistency residuals could be explored to identify discrepancies between measurements and estimated hydraulic behavior, without assuming that these discrepancies can unequivocally diagnose a sensor fault.

It is acknowledged that the existing literature already includes methods involving virtual sensors, pressure reconstruction, and residual-based anomaly detection. Therefore, the potential contribution of this extension would be experimental rather than methodological: to evaluate, for the specific 15-minute-ahead forecasting task in Area C, the relationship between forecast accuracy, dependence on the target sensor measurement, and the predictive capability of auxiliary hydraulic signals under controlled scenarios of telemetry unavailability or measurement faults.

Scope and Status: This hypothesis emerged after reviewing the Milestone 2 feedback and was not part of the originally submitted proposal. Contributions C1–C3 remain the primary objectives. The feasibility of this extension and its potential inclusion in the group project would be evaluated independently, depending on the time available. No auxiliary-only forecasting models, simulated fault scenarios, or residual-based indicators have yet been implemented or evaluated.
